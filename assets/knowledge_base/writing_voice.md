# Writing Voice — [CANDIDATE_NAME]

## How to generate this file

Claude can synthesize your voice from real samples of your writing. Run `/setup voice` and paste:
- 2-3 cover letters you've written in the past (the more authentic, the better)
- Optionally: 1-2 technical blog posts, LinkedIn articles, or essays you've written
- Optionally: responses to interview questions you've prepared

Claude will analyze your samples and generate this file with your specific patterns.

**Why this matters**: Without this file, the pipeline generates generic-sounding CVs and cover letters that don't match your voice. With it, outputs sound authentically like you — not like a template.

---

## What this file should contain (after running /setup voice)

### Tone Profile
- Your formality level (professional but warm? direct? methodical?)
- Whether you use first-person naturally or tend to avoid "I"
- Confidence level in your writing
- Any distinctive stylistic tendencies

### Sentence Patterns
- Typical sentence length and complexity
- Do you prefer short punchy sentences or longer explanatory ones?
- How do you handle technical terminology — define it, use it casually, or avoid jargon?

### Opening Strategies
Document 3-4 patterns you naturally use:
- e.g., "Start with a methodological insight" — "I've learned that..."
- e.g., "Direct introduction with a concrete claim" — "I've spent the last X years building..."

### Closing Strategies
How do you typically close a cover letter or email?
- Explicit call to action? Implicit invitation? Future direction?

### Vocabulary
- Words and phrases you naturally use
- Technical vocabulary preferences (do you say "deployed to production" or "shipped"?)
- What you actively avoid (clichés, buzzwords)

### Company Connection Patterns
How do you express genuine interest in a company?
- Do you cite their products? Their tech decisions? Their market position?
- What details do you typically research and reference?

### Metric and Achievement Framing
- Which types of metrics do you naturally cite?
- How do you describe impact without over-claiming?
- What scale references do you use? (record counts? user counts? time saved?)

### Bilingual Notes (if applicable)
If you write in both English and Spanish (or other languages):
- Does your tone shift between languages?
- Are there patterns specific to each language?
